business question : 
Are taxes and freight costs quietly eroding our profit margins, and does this impact our B2C (Internet) channel more than our B2B (Reseller) channel?

The B2B channel's extremely low product margin is not simply the result of aggregated data. A transaction-level analysis shows that many reseller sales are priced at or even below the standard product cost, which aligns with a wholesale pricing strategy. As a result, taxes and freight costs have a much greater impact because there is little product margin available to absorb these expenses. In contrast, the B2C channel benefits from higher retail markups, providing a margin buffer that is nearly four times larger than its tax and freight costs.
